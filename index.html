Summit Protocol

Find all five flags for a shot at a $150 swag shop credit.

Before you start

  • You must be here to play. Most flags require physical interaction at Bierstadt.
  • Flags will look like this: {ddrox-[A-Za-z]+-[A-Z0-9]{5}} — case, dashes, underscores, and spaces are all ignored when you type them in, so don't sweat the punctuation.
  • One stage at a time. Each flag you capture reveals the next riddle plus one line telling you where to submit at the end.
  • First three to get to the end of the trail win. Completing all five reveals a code. Take it to the submission form. First three submissions take a $150 credit to the DevOpsDays Rockies swag shop.
  • Don't wreck the venue. Nothing is hidden anywhere you'd need to climb, unscrew, open, or move. If it feels destructive, it's the wrong spot.
  • Don't spoil it. Share the win, not the flags.
